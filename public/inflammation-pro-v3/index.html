The 4 Pathways of Inflammation

Your body doesn't have ONE inflammation switch. It has four. And every supplement you've tried only touches one. Maybe.

01

NF-kB Pathway

The master switch. When NF-kB is overactive, it triggers a cascade of inflammatory signals throughout your entire body. Generic turmeric has less than 1% bioavailability... meaning 99% of what you swallow passes right through you. You're not taking turmeric. You're renting it.

02

COX-2 Pathway

The pain producer. This is what makes your joints ache and your muscles stiff. NSAIDs block this pathway, but they also block COX-1, which protects your stomach lining. Boswellia selectively targets COX-2 without the collateral damage.

03

Gut Barrier

The hidden trigger. 70% of your immune system lives in your gut. When your gut barrier is compromised, inflammatory particles leak into your bloodstream. This is called "leaky gut" and it's one of the most overlooked causes of systemic inflammation.

04

Oxidative Stress

The accelerant. Free radicals damage your cells and amplify every other inflammatory pathway. It's like pouring gasoline on a fire. Antioxidants neutralize these free radicals, slowing the entire inflammatory cascade.
