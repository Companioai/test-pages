Meriva® Curcumin is clinically shown to support a healthy inflammatory response via NF-κB pathway modulation.

NF-κB is a protein complex that acts as a master switch for inflammation. When chronically activated, it drives the production of inflammatory cytokines throughout your body. Meriva® Curcumin has been shown in multiple studies to help modulate this pathway, supporting a balanced inflammatory response.
